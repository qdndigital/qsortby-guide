The Analytics section shows what qSortBy is doing for your store. Its Overview tab rolls everything up: headline metrics at the top and a per-collection breakdown below. This guide walks through the cards, the date range, and the performance table.

## 1. Read the headline metrics

![Read the headline metrics](/assets/analytics-dashboard/qsortby-analytics-step-01.png)

Open **Analytics** from the left navigation. The Overview opens on four metric cards: **Collections managed**, **Avg. collection CTR**, **Auto-sorts run**, and **Revenue influenced**. Each shows its current value plus the change versus the previous period, so you can see the trend at a glance.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
These are mocked preview numbers — your store's real figures appear once qSortBy is installed and collecting data.
</Callout>

## 2. Set the date range

![Set the date range](/assets/analytics-dashboard/qsortby-analytics-step-02.png)

Every number on the page is scoped to a **date range** — shown here as **Last 30 days**. Change it to compare a busy launch week against a quieter month, or to check performance over a longer window. The cards and the table below both update together.

## 3. Compare collections in the table

![Compare collections in the table](/assets/analytics-dashboard/qsortby-analytics-step-03.png)

**Performance by collection** breaks the totals down row by row. Each collection shows its live signal, **CTR (30d)** with a sparkline **CTR trend**, views, revenue, and an **Uplift** figure — how much better it's doing since qSortBy took over sorting. Click any column header to sort by it.

That's the Analytics Overview: four headline cards, a date range that scopes the whole view, and a per-collection table where Uplift shows exactly what qSortBy's sorting is adding. Use the tabs at the top to dig into Customer events and the live Event log.
