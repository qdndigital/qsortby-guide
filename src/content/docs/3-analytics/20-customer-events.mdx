Beyond the headline metrics, Analytics lets you see the raw activity behind them. The Customer events tab lists the visitors qSortBy has seen, and the Event log streams live Shopify events as they happen. This guide covers both.

## 1. Open the Customer events tab

![Open the Customer events tab](/assets/customer-events/qsortby-customer-events-step-01.png)

Open **Analytics** from the left navigation, then select the **Customer events** tab — the trail is *Left menu → Analytics → Customer events*. It switches the page to a visitor-level view — every shopper qSortBy has tracked, one per row, instead of the rolled-up totals on Overview.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
These are mocked preview visitors — your real shoppers appear here once qSortBy is installed and tracking events.
</Callout>

## 2. Read the visitor table

![Read the visitor table](/assets/customer-events/qsortby-customer-events-step-02.png)

Each row is a visitor. The columns tell you what kind of shopper they are, the **device** they browsed on, and when they were **last seen**. Together they show who's active and how they're reaching your collections — useful context for the trends on the Overview tab.

## 3. Watch the live Event log

![Watch the live Event log](/assets/customer-events/qsortby-customer-events-step-03.png)

The **Event log** tab shows raw Shopify events as they arrive — a live stream of the activity qSortBy listens to. It's the ground truth behind every metric: if a signal looks off, this is where you confirm the underlying events are flowing.

That's the activity view: Customer events lists your visitors with kind, device and last-seen, while the Event log streams live Shopify events. Between them you can trace any headline metric back to the raw shopper behavior behind it.
