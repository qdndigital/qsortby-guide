Ranking answers *what sells*. It does not know that the refill belongs beside the candle, or that the trousers complete the shirt — so a sort that is right about every product individually can still scatter a set across the page. **Goes with** fixes that: a rule keeps related products **adjacent after ranking**, without changing which one ranks first.

A candle ranked 2nd and its refill ranked 37th end up next to each other, at the candle's position. The order the recipe decided is kept; the set is just gathered.

## Where it lives, and what it reaches

**Auto-sort → Goes with** lists your rules. A rule is **written once** and pointed at from as many collections as you like; editing it changes every collection pointing at it, and the table says how many. Switching a rule off on one collection changes nothing anywhere else.

A rule reaches two places:

- **A sorted collection's order**, where it gathers sets after the recipe has ranked.
- **The Goes-with rail on a product page** — a [theme block](/docs/auto-sort/theme-blocks) that shows a product's partners. Some rule types reach only one of the two; each page below says which.

Nothing in a rule ever changes what is **in** a collection. A Goes-with rule only moves products next to each other once the ranking has decided the order.

## Start from a suggestion, or write your own

The Goes-with tab offers **suggestions** drawn from your store — pairs that show up in the same orders, or products whose names and attributes say they belong together. Every suggestion says **how much it is worth trusting**: built from real orders, from a scan of your own catalogue, from names alone, or a guess. Take one, or write a rule from scratch; either way you end up in the same editor. See [Write a Goes-with rule](/docs/goes-with/write-a-rule).

<Callout type="note" title="A rule built from orders never re-resolves on its own">
That is deliberate. A rule you accepted from co-purchase data is a decision you made, and the app will not quietly change it under you when the orders shift. Instead, the **drift note** on the rule tells you when the orders behind it have gone quiet, and **Refresh** re-runs the detector and shows you the difference before anything is written.
</Callout>

## Plans

Every plan can write Goes-with rules and apply them to a sorted collection's order. **Publishing** the pairs to your products as metafields — so your theme or another app can render them without calling QSortby, and so the Goes-with rail reads them — starts on **Starter**. See [Publish to a product metafield](/docs/goes-with/publish-to-metafields).

## Glossary, briefly

- **Set** — products a rule has grouped, which travel together in a sorted collection.
- **Anchor / partner** — in a *Bring company to a product* rule, the anchor keeps its position and partners move to it.
- **Padded** — a slot filled by a best seller because there was not enough co-purchase data to pair honestly. Always labelled, and never counted back as evidence that the pair was real.
