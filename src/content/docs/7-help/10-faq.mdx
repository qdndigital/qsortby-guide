## Does it change my theme code?

No. Everything happens through the store's collection API. Your theme is never touched, so there's nothing to undo if you uninstall.

## How fast do changes appear?

The first sort can take a few minutes; after that, re-ranking is near real-time as orders come in.

## Which signal maximizes conversion — and how do I know?

There's no single magic setting. You pick the signal that fits each collection — best selling, top revenue, margin, **conversion rate**, trending, selling-fast and more (see [Choose a sort signal](/docs/collections/sort-signals)). To find the one that lifts *your* store, run an **A/B experiment**: qSortBy splits shoppers between your current order and the new sort, then reports click-through, add-to-cart and **influenced revenue** per variant with a statistical-significance verdict. See [Experiments & attribution](/docs/analytics/experiments-attribution).

## How do I know it's actually lifting sales?

Two places show it. A collection's **Activity** tab compares click-through before and after qSortBy and shows the uplift (see [Track collection activity](/docs/collections/collection-activity)). For the Best Sellers block and the For You feed, [Experiments & attribution](/docs/analytics/experiments-attribution) runs a live A/B test and attributes downstream revenue to the feed a shopper engaged with.

## It's not updating

A few things to check:

1. The collection is **enabled** in the app's Setup screen.
2. The collection has enough recent sales for a signal — brand-new collections need a little traffic first.
3. You're looking at the live storefront, not a cached preview.

## Can I keep a product at the top?

Yes — hand-set the order on a collection's **Manual order** tab; a product you place first stays first regardless of its score. See [Hand-curate the order](/docs/collections/manual-order).

## What happens if I uninstall?

The app stops reordering immediately and leaves your collections in their current order. No cleanup needed.
