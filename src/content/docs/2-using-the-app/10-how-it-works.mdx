The app continuously reorders each enabled collection so your best-performing products meet shoppers first — and out-of-stock items step aside.

## The ranking signals

Every product gets a score from a few signals, refreshed continuously:

- **Sales velocity** — units sold recently, weighted toward the last few days.
- **Conversion rate** — how often views turn into orders.
- **Stock health** — sold-out and low-stock items are pushed down automatically.

<Callout type="tip" title="Sensible defaults">
The default weighting works well for most stores. You only need to change it if you have a specific merchandising goal.
</Callout>

## Tuning the weights

You can bias the ranking from the app's **Settings** screen:

```json
{
  "weights": { "sales": 0.6, "conversion": 0.3, "freshness": 0.1 },
  "demoteSoldOut": true,
  "pinned": ["new-arrivals-hero"]
}
```

- Raise `sales` to reward proven sellers.
- Raise `freshness` to give new products more visibility.
- Add product handles to `pinned` to lock them to the top.

## Pinning and excluding

Sometimes you want manual control. Two options:

1. **Pin** a product to keep it at the top regardless of score.
2. **Exclude** a collection entirely — the app won't touch it.
