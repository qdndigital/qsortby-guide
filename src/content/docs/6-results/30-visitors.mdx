**Results → Visitors** (Pro) lists the visitors QSortby has served — all time by default, or only those last seen in the range you pick at the top of the page. It exists so you can answer one question — *why did this shopper see that order?* — and it is built so that answering it never requires knowing who they are.

## What is stored, and what is not

Behaviour is stored; identity is not. An anonymous visitor is **an id, what they looked at, and what they bought**. No email is kept. A shopper who **signs in** is matched to their Shopify customer so their history carries across devices, and is shown under their Shopify name; nothing else about the person is kept.

<Callout type="note" title="Privacy requests are honoured in full">
A customer data erasure request from Shopify deletes that visitor's events along with the rest of their data, device by device. There is nothing to reconstruct afterwards.
</Callout>

## The list

Filter by **visitor** (everyone, bought something, known customers, guests), **device** and **browser**, and sort by last seen or most events; **Search visitors** finds a visitor or a customer. Each row shows the visitor (with their place, where known), customer or guest, where they came from, events, top action, device, and when they were last and first seen. *No visitor activity yet* on a new install means the theme app embed has not reported a visit — [Setup](/docs/getting-started/setup) step 1.

## One visitor

Click a visitor's name for their page:

**Timeline** — the events in order, grouped by day: pages viewed, products opened, cart adds and removals, orders, and which **surface** served each product they opened. Where a product came from a QSortby surface, the row says which one — the collection, the For You feed, an upsell — which is the "why" behind an influenced order.

**How they decided** — how many different products they looked at, added to a cart and bought, and what Intent AI read each visit as.

Beside it: their **orders**, and whether QSortby is credited with influencing each; **the last ranking they were served**, when one was recorded, with each product's score and raw signal readings — not the weighted shares [Why this order](/docs/personalization/why-this-order) shows for a simulated shopper; **what they did**, counted by event kind; and **products** — looked at most, put in a cart, recently viewed, and anything they have **hidden** (a product they dismissed from a feed is never shown to them again).

**Taste profile** — what the app has inferred for ranking: the categories, attributes, moods, seasons and occasions they lean to. **Built from** says how many signals it rests on (purchases and product views of products carrying a product-data value), so a profile built from one visit is read as one visit.

## What it is for

Two things, mostly. Checking a rule against reality — *"first-time visitors should be landing in group 2; are they?"* — and understanding a surprising order before you change a setting on a hunch. It is not a CRM, and it does not try to be one.
