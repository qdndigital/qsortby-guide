**Auto-sort** is the main job of the app, and the first entry in the menu after Home. Its **Collections** tab lists every collection QSortby keeps sorted and what each is sorted by, with a **Status** filter (Live, Test mode, Paused, Needs setup and the rest) above the list. Three more tabs sit beside it — [Products](/docs/auto-sort/products-tab), [Personalization](/docs/personalization/create-a-rule) and [Goes with](/docs/goes-with/goes-with-overview) — because all three are ways of deciding an order, not separate places.

There are two ways to get a collection in here, and they differ on one thing: **who decides which products are in it**.

## Auto-sort a collection you already have

Your Shopify collections are listed on the Collections tab once the install import finishes (**Sync collections** brings in ones you made since). Open one, choose an order under **Sort products by** and save — or, on a first install, use **Sort it now** in [Setup](/docs/getting-started/setup). Reversible. QSortby:

1. **Saves a snapshot** of the order the collection has right now, so there is always a way back.
2. **Writes the new order to Shopify**, and sets the collection's sort order to **Manual** so Shopify holds the order instead of recomputing its own.
3. **Keeps it fresh** — re-sorts on the schedule you choose, and whenever a new order lands.

Only the **order** changes. Smart sorting on a collection you already have never adds or removes a product, because you own the membership. Products the recipe can score rise to the top; products it cannot score keep their relative order below them; sold-out products go to the end unless you tell it otherwise.

<Callout type="warning" title="This changes what shoppers see on your storefront">
The app says this sentence before every live write, and it means it: a few minutes after QSortby writes the new order, shoppers see it. If you would rather look first, switch on [test mode](/docs/auto-sort/test-mode-and-preview) on the collection instead — QSortby computes the order on schedule without publishing it, and can show it to you in your own theme through a temporary preview collection.
</Callout>

## Create a collection the app runs

**Auto-sort a collection** opens the other door: the **Create collection** page. A collection created here is **run by the app**: QSortby picks which products are in it from your criteria and keeps that membership up to date, as well as the order. That is the one thing it does that auto-sorting an existing collection does not.

- **Name it**, then choose what to rank by and the window to count.
- **Set how many products it should hold.** The app fills it from your criteria and keeps it filled — a "Top 24 this week" that is always exactly 24 products, re-chosen on every sort.
- Only products that are **Active** and **published to the Online Store** are eligible. Drafts and archived products are never promoted; sold-out products stay eligible and sink to the end.
- It starts in [test mode](/docs/auto-sort/test-mode-and-preview) (a Personalize collection does not): the collection exists in Shopify but empty, and the order is computed but not published until you press **Apply to live** on its **Test results** tab.
- To hand-pick the products instead, create it and switch membership to **Manual** on the collection page; the app then only reorders what you put in.

A **Personalized** collection is a special case of this: on Shopify it holds your best sellers, the same for everyone, so theme sections such as *Featured collection* still show products — and the **For You** theme block renders a different order per shopper on top of it. See [How Personalize collections work](/docs/how-it-works/ranking-personalize).

## The collection page

Open any row and you land on the collection's **Setup** tab, which answers one question: *what order does this collection run, and why does each product sit where it does?*

- **Sort by** — the recipe, and the window it counts. An **In plain words** card restates the setting as a sentence: *"Best selling, based on sales from the last 30 days, sold-out products last."* The noun changes with the recipe — *launched in* for Newest arrivals, *reviewed in* for Top rated, *activity from* for an engagement metric — because those recipes count something other than sales.
- **Keep it updated** — how often it re-sorts and whether the result is pushed to Shopify automatically. See [Set the time window & keep it fresh](/docs/auto-sort/window-and-schedule).
- **Sold-out products** — what the recipe does with products that are out of stock when it sorts — **Show last** (the default), **Keep in their ranked position**, or **Hide from this collection** (offered only where the app runs the membership).
- **Products** — **Max products** caps how many the collection promotes; **No limit** ranks and promotes every product in it.
- **Advanced** — one setting, closed by default: **Give under-measured products a chance to be seen** (up to three products a day, in positions 6 to 20).
- **Intent suggest** — on a Personalize collection, lets the [intent read](/docs/upsell/intent-suggest) nudge the order for shoppers it has read. Off by default.

Beside Setup sit **Preview** (a dry run you look at once) and **Test results** (test mode, a state), and a link to **Snapshots**. The products table below the settings shows the order the recipe produces right now, with the metric it ranked each product on.

<Callout type="note" title="Nothing is written the moment you change a setting">
Saving the recipe or the window does not re-sort on the spot. The change is applied by the collection's next scheduled sort. Press **Resync now** if you want it this minute.
</Callout>

## Leaving

The bottom of the Setup tab has a **Danger zone** card with **Remove from QSortby**: it stops the app touching the collection, which stays in Shopify with its products. The dialog asks what to leave behind, each spelled out before it happens:

- **Put the products back in the order they were in before QSortby** — the collection stays on Manual, holding that order, and you maintain it from then on.
- **Hand the collection back to Shopify's … sort** — Shopify maintains the order again and recomputes QSortby's away.
- Neither — the collection stays on Manual with whatever order it has. Ticking **Also delete this collection in Shopify** removes the page and every link to it, and the app asks you to type the collection's name before it will.

Your products are never deleted by any of these. They just stop being ordered, or grouped, by QSortby.
