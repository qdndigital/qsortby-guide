**Auto-sort → Products** is your catalogue with live behaviour attached: every product QSortby tracks, and beside each one the numbers the recipes rank by. It is not a report — Results is for that. It is where you work out **what a collection should be sorted by**, before you commit it to a recipe.

## Two questions the same table answers

A product holding at **168 units a month** is a best seller. A product that went **from 4 to 11 units this week** is trending. Both are worth showing, and they need different recipes. Pick a criterion and a window at the top of the table and it re-ranks to that question:

- **Best selling** over 30 days shows you the steady earners.
- **Trending** over 7 days shows you the climbers — rate of change, not total.
- **Most impressions · Most clicks · Highest CTR · Most added to cart · Longest dwell · Best converting** show what shoppers *did* with a product, from the storefront pixel.
- **Top rated** ranks on reviews, where a review app QSortby can read is installed.

Each column header explains what it measures on hover. **Sold** comes from your real orders; the engagement columns come from the pixel, so they are empty until the theme app embed is on and shoppers have moved through the store.

<Callout type="note" title="A short window on a new shop is thin">
The numbers come from imported orders, so a 7-day window on a shop that installed yesterday has seven days of data at most, however old the shop is. **Reading your store** on Settings → Tracking says how far back the import reached; until it has finished, prefer a longer window.
</Callout>

## Applying the answer is a separate act

The table changes nothing on your storefront. Once you can see which question a collection should answer, you apply it on the collection itself:

- Open the collection from the **Collections** tab and change its recipe, or
- Tick several collections in the list and **Apply a recipe** to the batch — see [Manage your collections](/docs/auto-sort/manage-collections).

*New Arrivals* is the usual home for **Trending now**, re-sorting hourly; a large evergreen collection is usually better on **Best selling** over 30 or 90 days.

## Filters and saved views

**+ Add filter** narrows the table by status, category, price and inventory; **Search** finds a product by name. A filter-and-sort combination can be kept as a named **view** tab — "Low stock, selling fast" is a useful one — so a question you ask every Monday is one click.

## Open a product

Click any row to open the product: where it ranks in every collection it is in, what would move it, what it travels with and why. See [One product: what it sells with, and why](/docs/auto-sort/product-detail).
