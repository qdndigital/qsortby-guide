**Auto-sort → Collections** is mission control. Every collection shows a status, its sort rule, its schedule, and when it last sorted — so you can see what's working and step in when needed.

## 1. Read statuses at a glance

![Read statuses at a glance](/assets/manage-collections/collections-manage-step-01.png)

The tiles up top count your collections in total and by state: **Live** (actively sorting), **Needs setup** (no rule chosen yet), and **Paused** (a rule is set, automatic updates are off). In the table, each row shows the same status plus its **Sort Rule**, **Schedule** (Real-time / Hourly / Daily / Manual), and **Last sorted** time.

## 2. Filter by source

![Filter by source](/assets/manage-collections/collections-manage-step-02.png)

The tabs filter the list: **All**, **From Shopify** (collections you imported), **From QSortby** (created here), and **Personalize collections** (per-visitor For You feeds). Use the **Status** filter to narrow by Live / Paused / Needs setup / Archived.

## 3. Apply one recipe to several collections

Tick the collections you want and press **Apply a recipe**. You choose one recipe **and its window** — the same "Best selling" over 7 days and over 90 days are two different orders — and everything ticked gets exactly that. It **overwrites** what each collection runs today, which is why today's setting is printed beside every row: untick anything running something deliberate before you confirm, because this is the last place you can see it.

Schedules, product caps and Goes-with rules are left alone; only the recipe changes. And applying does not re-sort anything on the spot — each collection picks the new recipe up on its own next sort.

## 4. Bring in Shopify collections

![Import Shopify collections](/assets/manage-collections/collections-manage-step-03.png)

Collections you already have in Shopify are listed as soon as the install import finishes; **Sync all from Shopify** refreshes the list if you've created new ones since. Auto-sorting a collection you already have only ever **reorders**: the app never adds or removes products, because you own the membership. The products your rule scores rise to the top, products with no score keep their current relative order below them, and anything sold out, draft or archived sinks to the bottom. To pause or resume a collection, open it and use **Pause smart sort** / **Resume smart sort** on its detail page.

<Callout type="note" title="Keeping a product out of every collection">
**Settings → Excluded products** keeps a product out of every app-managed collection; a collection's own excluded list keeps it out of just that one. Exclusions beat ranking — an excluded product never appears, however well it scores, and it's dropped from top-up filler too.
</Callout>
