**Auto-sort → Collections** is mission control. Every collection shows its recipe, how often it re-sorts, what it reached and earned, and a status — so you can see what's working and step in when needed.

## 1. Read statuses at a glance

![Read statuses at a glance](/assets/manage-collections/collections-manage-step-01.png)

Each row shows its **Surface**, **Recipe**, **Re-sorts** (*on order*, *every 1h*, *every 6h*, *every day*, *manual*, or *On changes* for a sold-out rule waiting on stock), **Reaches**, **Earning** and **Status**. A live row's status says when it last sorted — *Live · 12m ago*. The other states include **Needs setup** (nothing runs here yet), **Paused** (a recipe is set, automatic updates are off), **Test mode**, **Over plan limit**, **No slots left**, **Archived** and **Deleted in Shopify**.

## 2. Filter by source

![Filter by source](/assets/manage-collections/collections-manage-step-02.png)

Two filters sit above the list: **Source** — **Any source**, **From Shopify** (collections you imported) or **From QSortby** (created here) — and **Status**, to narrow by any of the states above. **Search** finds a collection by name.

## 3. Apply one recipe to several collections

Tick the collections you want and press **Apply a recipe**. You choose one recipe **and its window** — the same "Best selling" over 7 days and over 90 days are two different orders — and everything ticked gets exactly that. It **overwrites** what each collection runs today, which is why today's setting is printed beside every row: untick anything running something deliberate before you confirm, because this is the last place you can see it.

Schedules, product caps and Goes-with rules are left alone; only the recipe changes. And applying does not re-sort anything on the spot — each collection picks the new recipe up on its own next sort.

## 4. Bring in Shopify collections

![Import Shopify collections](/assets/manage-collections/collections-manage-step-03.png)

Collections you already have in Shopify are listed as soon as the install import finishes; **Sync collections** refreshes the list if you've created new ones since. Auto-sorting a collection you already have only ever **reorders**: the app never adds or removes products, because you own the membership. The products your rule scores rise to the top, products with no score keep their current relative order below them, and anything sold out, draft or archived sinks to the bottom. To pause or resume a collection, open it and use **Pause smart sort** / **Resume smart sort** on its detail page.

<Callout type="note" title="Keeping a product out of every collection">
**Excluded products** (Settings → Product data & AI) keeps a product out of every app-managed collection; a collection's own excluded list keeps it out of just that one. Exclusions beat ranking — an excluded product never appears, however well it scores, and it's dropped from top-up filler too.
</Callout>
