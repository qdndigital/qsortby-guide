**Settings → AI connections** lets an AI assistant you already use read your store's QSortby data over **MCP**, the open connector standard Claude and ChatGPT both speak. You ask the assistant in plain words; it asks QSortby; QSortby answers with the same honesty rules as its own screens. Pro and Growth; rolling out, so the tab may say *QSortby has not opened AI connections yet* for a while — you can still set who may connect.

## What an assistant can read

The same things you can read in the app, through eight tools:

- your **collections** and how each is sorted;
- **why products rank** where they do;
- **influenced revenue**, with the same last-touch caveat the app prints;
- products **moving up or down**;
- your **experiments** and their verdicts;
- **what shoppers came for** — the intent mix.

It never sees customer personal data: the tools answer with aggregates and product-level facts, and every answer is a fixed allowlist of fields, so a question phrased cleverly cannot pull out more than the screen would show.

## What an assistant can set up

Where the store allows it, the assistant can also prepare things **for you to review** — never anything live. It needs both the store-wide policy below and the connection's own switch in its row, **Read only · Allow changes**, which starts off and only the store owner can turn on:

- **collections**, created in [test mode](/docs/auto-sort/test-mode-and-preview) — empty in Shopify, with nothing sorted onto them until you apply the test (not personalized collections);
- **personalization rules**, as drafts;
- **Goes-with rules**, switched off;
- **upsell offers**, created and configured but switched off.

The assistant shows you each of these and waits for your go-ahead in the chat; nothing it sets up is used on your storefront until you publish or apply it. It can also **ask to start or stop an A/B test** — those do not run at once: they wait under *Waiting for your approval* on the AI connections tab, with **Approve** and **Reject** beside each, for up to 24 hours, until someone allowed to make changes approves.

## Connecting

Each store has its own connector address, shown on the tab. Add it to Claude as a **custom connector**, or to ChatGPT in **developer mode**; the assistant sends you to sign in to your Shopify store, QSortby checks that you are allowed to connect, and you confirm what the assistant may do. The connection then appears in the list with the name the assistant gave it and who connected it, and **Revoke** disconnects it.

## Who may connect, and who may let it write

Two policies, both changeable **only by the store owner**:

- **Who may connect an assistant** — only the store owner; the owner and staff; or the owner, staff and collaborator accounts. Narrowing it disconnects anyone it no longer allows.
- **Who may let an assistant make changes** — no one (assistants only read); only when the store owner connected it; when the owner or staff connected it; or when the owner, staff or a collaborator connected it. It applies to the next change an assistant tries; existing connections stay connected.

<Callout type="note" title="Why the defaults are tight">
A connection is a standing permission, and a read-only one is harmless at worst. Writing — even as drafts — is owner-only by default because an assistant that drafts twenty collections overnight is a cleanup job, and the person who pays for the store should be the one who opened that door.
</Callout>

## What it cannot do

Change anything live. Read another store. See a shopper's email or name. Be connected by an account the policy does not allow. If the plan stops including it, or the app is uninstalled, every connection is refused from that moment — the gate fails closed.
