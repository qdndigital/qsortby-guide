The cart upsell is the widest-reach offer you can run: every shopper who opens a cart sees it. It suggests products drawn from your live ranking, skipping anything already in the basket and anything out of stock.

Included on **every plan**, including Free.

## Choose what it suggests

**Rank by** picks the signal — best sellers, trending, selling fast, most profitable, top revenue, customer favorites, top rated, newest, or an engagement metric such as best converting (a personalization logic can't be used here) — and **Sales window** sets how far back to measure. Short windows chase trends; longer ones are steadier on a low-volume store.

Switch **Product selection** to **Manual** to hand-pick products instead. A manual list shows exactly what you chose, in your order, and is deliberately *not* topped up from the ranker: you picked a list, so a short list stays short.

<Callout type="note" title="A short list gets padded, not truncated">
In automatic mode, if the ranking can't fill the widget — a small catalogue, or most winners already in the cart — QSortby tops it up with your newest in-stock products rather than rendering one lonely card. The dashboard tells you when a preview is padded rather than purely ranked, so you always know which you're looking at.
</Callout>

## Choose where it appears

The cart drawer and the cart page are configured **separately**, on their own tabs, because they're different amounts of space. For each one you set:

- **On / off** — run it in the drawer only, the page only, or both.
- **Placement** — a **Theme preset** (or *Detect automatically*), or your own **Anchor element** as a CSS selector, and a **Position** — before or after that element, or inside it at the top or bottom.
- **Card style** — **App** cards (QSortby's own styling, works everywhere) or **Theme** cards (renders through one of your theme's own product snippets so it inherits your design).
- **Products to show** — the number of suggestions. Fewer is usually better in a drawer.

The cart page also takes a **Layout** — grid or slider, and how many cards across on desktop and mobile. The drawer is always a single column.

## Excluded products

Products you've excluded in [**Settings**](/docs/settings/settings-overview) never appear in the cart, popup, free-shipping, thank-you or in-checkout offers, even if you hand-pick them in manual mode. That list is the single place to keep something out of all of them at once. The one exception is a real pair on the Frequently bought together rail, which comes straight from your orders and is not filtered by that list.

## Running more than one

A second cart upsell is how you run a different offer for carts over a certain value, or for carts holding something from a collection. When several are switched on, QSortby walks them in **priority** order and serves the first whose [display rules](/docs/upsell/display-rules) match the cart as it stands. An upsell with no rules attached matches every cart. The winner decides what is suggested; where the widget sits (drawer and page on/off, anchors, card style) comes from the top-priority live cart upsell, so the drawer and the cart page show the same offer.

Your plan caps how many you can create — one on Free, three on Starter, unlimited above.
