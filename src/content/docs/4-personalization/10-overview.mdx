Personalization lets qSortBy show a different product order to different shoppers — repeat buyers see the categories they love, first-time visitors see your best sellers. You do this with segment rules. This overview covers the rule list, what Published vs Draft means, and how to start a new rule.

## 1. Meet the Segment Rules list

![Meet the Segment Rules list](/assets/overview/qsortby-personalization-overview-step-01.png)

Open **Segment Rules** from the left menu. Each rule reorders your collections based on **who** is shopping — its own audience conditions and ranking signals. The list shows every rule you've built, with its **status**, how many **shopper groups** it targets, and when it was last updated.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
These are mocked preview rules — your own rules appear here once the app is installed.
</Callout>

## 2. Published vs Draft

![Published vs Draft](/assets/overview/qsortby-personalization-overview-step-02.png)

Every rule carries a **status**. A **Published** rule is live and personalizing your storefront right now. A **Draft** is saved but not yet affecting shoppers — build and refine it safely until you're ready. Use the **All / Published / Draft** filters above the table to focus the list.

## 3. Start a new rule

![Start a new rule](/assets/overview/qsortby-personalization-overview-step-03.png)

Select **Create segment rule** in the top-right to begin. qSortBy asks how you'd like to start: **describe your shoppers to AI** and let it build the groups, pick a **ready-made preset**, or **build from scratch** by hand. Whichever you choose, you land in the same rule editor and can adjust everything afterward.

That's the lay of the land: a list of segment rules, each Published (live) or Draft (safe to edit), and a single Create segment rule entry that offers three ways to start. Next, pick the start method that suits you.
