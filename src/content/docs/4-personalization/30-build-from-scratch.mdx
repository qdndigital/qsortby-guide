Building from scratch gives you full control over exactly who a rule targets and how their products are ranked. You'll name the rule, add a shopper group, define its audience with conditions, choose ranking signals and how much each one matters, and see the locked Everyone else fallback that catches everyone you didn't target.

## 1. Name the rule

![Name the rule](/assets/build-from-scratch/qsortby-segment-scratch-step-01.png)

Open **Segment Rules** from the left menu, then **Create segment rule** and choose **build from scratch**. Give the rule a clear **name** so you'll recognize it in the list later — something like *Returning vs. new shoppers*. The rule opens as a **Draft** and autosaves, so nothing goes live while you build. Not sure what to call it? Select **Suggest**.

## 2. Add a shopper group

![Add a shopper group](/assets/build-from-scratch/qsortby-segment-scratch-step-02.png)

A **shopper group** is one audience you want to treat differently. Select **Add a shopper group** to create one — you can add several, each with its own audience and ranking. Below your groups sits **Everyone else**, the catch-all that handles every shopper a group didn't match.

## 3. Define the audience

![Define the audience](/assets/build-from-scratch/qsortby-segment-scratch-step-03.png)

Inside a group, build its audience from **conditions**. Each condition is three parts: an **Attribute** (like *Past purchases* or *Days since last visit*), a **Comparison** (*is at least*, *is more than*…), and a **Value**. Select **Add condition** to stack more — a shopper must match them all to fall into this group.

## 4. Pick ranking signals and their importance

![Pick ranking signals and their importance](/assets/build-from-scratch/qsortby-segment-scratch-step-04.png)

Now decide how this group's products are ordered. Under **Ranking**, choose **signals** — *Repeat purchase*, *Recently viewed*, *Top sellers* and more — and set how much each one matters with **Set importance %**. Higher importance pulls that signal harder. Select **Add signal** to blend several.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
Blend signals rather than relying on one — e.g. Repeat purchase weighted high, Favorite category affinity as a lighter boost.
</Callout>

## 5. The Everyone else fallback

![The Everyone else fallback](/assets/build-from-scratch/qsortby-segment-scratch-step-05.png)

At the bottom sits **Everyone else** — a locked catch-all you can't delete. Any shopper who doesn't match one of your groups is ranked here, by default with **Top sellers** then **New arrivals**. Because it always covers the remainder, every shopper gets a sensible order even before you add more groups.

You've built a rule by hand: named it, added a targeted shopper group with audience conditions and weighted ranking signals, and seen how Everyone else catches the rest. Next, review the live preview and publish it.
