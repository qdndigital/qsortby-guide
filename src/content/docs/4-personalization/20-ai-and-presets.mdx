You don't have to build a rule by hand. qSortBy offers two fast starts: describe your shoppers in plain English and let AI create the groups for you, or pick a ready-made preset and tweak it. This guide walks through both.

## 1. Describe your shoppers to AI

![Describe your shoppers to AI](/assets/ai-and-presets/qsortby-segment-ai-presets-step-01.png)

Open **Segment Rules** from the left menu, then **Create segment rule**. With the **Describe your shoppers** start, you just type who you want to reach and what they should see — for example, *reward repeat buyers with categories they love, and show new visitors my best sellers*. Write it the way you'd explain it to a colleague; AI turns it into shopper groups and ranking signals.

## 2. Generate the rule

![Generate the rule](/assets/ai-and-presets/qsortby-segment-ai-presets-step-02.png)

Select **Generate** and AI builds the shopper groups, audience conditions, and ranking signals from your description. Everything it creates is fully editable — treat it as a strong first draft, then fine-tune any group by hand. Prefer to keep going yourself? Select **or build it manually** instead.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
Use Refine writing to tidy your description before generating, or Suggest to name the rule.
</Callout>

## 3. Or start from a preset

![Or start from a preset](/assets/ai-and-presets/qsortby-segment-ai-presets-step-03.png)

Prefer a proven starting point? The **preset** start loads a ready-made shopper group — here, **Returning customers** — already wired with audience conditions and ranking signals. Use **Start from a template** to swap in another preset, then adjust the audience and signals to fit your store.

Both fast starts drop you into the same editor: AI builds from your description, presets give you a proven template. From here you can adjust every group, or switch to building from scratch for full control.
