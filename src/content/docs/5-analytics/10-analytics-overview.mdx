Analytics shows how shoppers engage with your ranked collections. The **Overview** tab summarises impressions, clicks and click-through rate, then breaks it down per collection with a trend line — so you can see what's working. Five more tabs sit beside it, covering revenue impact, movers, per-config history, the For You funnel and the raw visitor list.

Analytics is a **Pro** feature. (Every new install runs on the Growth feature set for its first 10 days — see [Your first 10 days](/docs/getting-started/reverse-trial).)

## 1. Open Analytics

![Open Analytics](/assets/analytics-overview/analytics-overview-step-01.png)

Open **Analytics** from the left menu. The cards up top total your **impressions**, **clicks**, **overall CTR** and how many **collections** are tracked over the window you choose (top-right).

## 2. Read performance by collection

![Read performance by collection](/assets/analytics-overview/analytics-overview-step-02.png)

The **Performance by collection** table ranks each collection by CTR, with the **signal** it's sorted by, a little **CTR trend** sparkline (green = rising, red = falling), and its views, clicks and product count. It's the fastest way to see which sorted collections are earning their clicks.

## 3. The other four tabs

Overview is the entry point; four more tabs sit beside it and load when you open them.

- **Impact** — influenced orders and revenue against your store totals, with influenced AOV next to overall AOV and a daily series.
- **Trending** — products rising and falling on revenue, with the change and growth rate behind each move.
- **Sort projects** — what each ranking config earned *while it was live*: attributed orders, revenue, AOV and feed CTR per config version, so a sort you've since changed is still judged on its own window.
- **Personalization** — the For You feed's own funnel: impressions, clicks, cart adds, orders and revenue, split by device and by A/B variant.

## 4. Switch to Customer events

![Switch to Customer events](/assets/analytics-overview/analytics-overview-step-03.png)

The **Customer events** tab lists individual visitors — filter by device, browser or type, and click any row to see that shopper's full event timeline. Great for understanding behaviour behind the numbers.
