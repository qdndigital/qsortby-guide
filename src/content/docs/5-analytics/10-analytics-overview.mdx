Analytics shows how shoppers engage with your ranked collections. The **Overview** tab summarises impressions, clicks and click-through rate, then breaks it down per collection with a trend line — so you can see what's working. (Analytics is a Pro feature.)

## 1. Open Analytics

![Open Analytics](/assets/analytics-overview/analytics-overview-step-01.png)

Open **Analytics** from the left menu. The cards up top total your **impressions**, **clicks**, **overall CTR** and how many **collections** are tracked over the window you choose (top-right).

## 2. Read performance by collection

![Read performance by collection](/assets/analytics-overview/analytics-overview-step-02.png)

The **Performance by collection** table ranks each collection by CTR, with a little **CTR trend** sparkline (green = rising, red = falling) plus its views, clicks and product count. It's the fastest way to see which sorted collections are earning their clicks.

## 3. Switch to Customer events

![Switch to Customer events](/assets/analytics-overview/analytics-overview-step-03.png)

The **Customer events** tab lists individual visitors — filter by device, browser or type, and click any row to see that shopper's full event timeline. Great for understanding behaviour behind the numbers.
