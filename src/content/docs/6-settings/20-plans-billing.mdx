The **Plans** page lays out qSortBy's four tiers side by side. This guide helps you compare them, understand the free trial, and pick the plan that fits your store.

## 1. Compare the tiers

![Compare the tiers](/assets/plans-billing/qsortby-plans-step-01.png)

Open **Plans & Billing** from the left menu. qSortBy comes in four tiers — **Starter**, **Growth**, **Pro** and **Enterprise** — shown side by side with what each includes. Read down a column to see how collection limits, signals and automation scale as you move up.

## 2. Spot the most popular plan

![Spot the most popular plan](/assets/plans-billing/qsortby-plans-step-02.png)

One tier is badged **Most popular** — it's the plan most stores land on because it balances price against the signals and automation most catalogs need. It's a safe default if you're unsure where to start.

## 3. Start your free trial

![Start your free trial](/assets/plans-billing/qsortby-plans-step-03.png)

Every paid tier opens with a **7-day free trial**, so you can try qSortBy on your real catalog before you're billed. Select **Start 7-day free trial** on the plan you want — you can switch tiers later from **Settings → Current plan**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
You can change or cancel your plan any time from Settings — the trial only starts billing once it ends.
</Callout>

That's plans and billing: compare the four tiers, lean on the Most popular badge if you're unsure, and start risk-free with the 7-day free trial.
