The **Integrations** page links qSortBy to the third-party tools that feed it richer signals. This guide covers managing a connected Klaviyo account and connecting Google Analytics 4.

## 1. Review your Klaviyo connection

![Review your Klaviyo connection](/assets/integrations/qsortby-integrations-step-01.png)

Open **Integrations** from the left menu. **Klaviyo** shows here as **connected**. The card lists the linked **account** and the **granted scopes** — the exact permissions qSortBy was given. Select **Disconnect** if you ever need to revoke access and unlink the account.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
Review the granted scopes before you rely on an integration — they tell you exactly what data qSortBy can read.
</Callout>

## 2. Connect Google Analytics 4

![Connect Google Analytics 4](/assets/integrations/qsortby-integrations-step-02.png)

**Google Analytics 4** is not linked yet. Select **Connect** on its card to start the OAuth flow — you'll sign in to Google and approve the scopes qSortBy needs. Once connected, its engagement data feeds into your sorts alongside Shopify's.

That's integrations: keep an eye on your connected Klaviyo account and its scopes, and connect Google Analytics 4 to fold its data into qSortBy's ranking.
