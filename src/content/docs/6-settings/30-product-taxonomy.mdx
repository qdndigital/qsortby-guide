The **Product taxonomy** editor defines the dimensions qSortBy uses to understand and group your catalog. This guide walks you through reading a dimension, adding your own, and saving the taxonomy back to your store.

## 1. Read a dimension

![Read a dimension](/assets/product-taxonomy/qsortby-taxonomy-step-01.png)

This editor lives one level in: **Left menu → Settings → Product taxonomy**. It ships with five dimensions — **Category**, **Material**, **Season**, **Occasion** and **Fit**. Each maps to a Shopify **metafield key**, is either **single-** or **multi-select**, and lists its **allowed values**. A **catalog-coverage bar** shows how many of your products already carry that data.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
Higher coverage means richer sorts — fill the matching metafields on your products in Shopify admin to raise it.
</Callout>

## 2. Add a dimension — or let AI suggest one

![Add a dimension — or let AI suggest one](/assets/product-taxonomy/qsortby-taxonomy-step-02.png)

Select **Add dimension** to create your own — give it a name, a metafield key, choose single- or multi-select, and list its allowed values. Short on ideas? Choose **Suggest with AI** and qSortBy proposes dimensions and values based on your actual catalog.

## 3. Save the taxonomy

![Save the taxonomy](/assets/product-taxonomy/qsortby-taxonomy-step-03.png)

When your dimensions look right, select **Save taxonomy**. qSortBy provisions the matching metafield definitions in your Shopify admin so you (or your team) can start filling them in per product — and the coverage bars climb as you do.

That's the taxonomy editor: read the built-in dimensions, add your own or generate them with AI, then save to provision the metafields. The richer and more complete your taxonomy, the sharper qSortBy's sorting.
