The Products page is your catalog with live behaviour attached. It's where you inspect how each product performs before deciding which signal to rank a collection by. Metrics come from the storefront pixel and your orders.

## 1. Open Products

![Open Products](/assets/explore-products/products-explore-step-01.png)

Open **Products** from the left menu. Each row shows the product plus its engagement over your chosen window: **Sold**, **Views**, **Clicks**, **CTR**, **Cart adds** and **Avg dwell**. **Sold** comes from your real orders; the other five come from the storefront pixel. Click **Sync products** (top right) to pull the latest catalog from Shopify.

## 2. Sort by any signal

![Sort by any signal](/assets/explore-products/products-explore-step-02.png)

Use **Criteria** to sort by a metric — Best selling, Most impressions, Most clicks, Highest CTR, Most added to cart, Longest dwell. Set the window (Hour / Day / Custom range / Period) beside it. The same signals drive collection ranking, so this is a great place to preview what a rule would surface.

## 3. Filter and save views

![Filter and save views](/assets/explore-products/products-explore-step-03.png)

Add filters (status, category, price, inventory) with **+ Add filter**, then keep a filter+sort combination as a named **view** tab for one-click access later. Hover any metric column header to see exactly what it measures.
