When you install QSortby, a short setup wizard gets your store ready in five guided steps. You can complete it in one sitting or come back to it any time — it always resumes at your first unfinished step. This guide walks through each step and how to move between them.

## 1. Open setup from the Dashboard

![Open setup from the Dashboard](/assets/setup-wizard/getting-started-setup-step-01.png)

When you install QSortby a short **setup wizard** gets your store ready in five guided steps. You can reach it any time and it always resumes at your first unfinished step. Here we'll walk through each step.

## 2. Follow your progress

![Follow your progress](/assets/setup-wizard/getting-started-setup-step-02.png)

The wizard runs across **five steps**, and the header shows which one you're on with a progress bar and a percentage of the way through. Three of them are the ones that matter — industry, pixel, tracking — and they carry a **To do** or **Done** badge. The last two are marked **Optional** and are ticked from the start: adding the storefront blocks and picking a plan are things you can do whenever you like, and neither blocks you from finishing.

## 3. Step 1 — Tell us your industry

![Step 1 — Tell us your industry](/assets/setup-wizard/getting-started-setup-step-03.png)

Pick the industry that best matches your store. QSortby builds a starting product taxonomy and the matching Shopify metafields — the vocabulary personalization uses to describe your catalogue, and what your [emotional keyword set](/docs/settings/settings-overview) is generated against. Click **Continue** when done.

## 4. Steps 2 & 3 — Turn on tracking

![Steps 2 & 3 — Turn on tracking](/assets/setup-wizard/getting-started-setup-step-04.png)

**Step 2** enables the **QSortby pixel** on your storefront — open the theme editor, toggle *QSortby Pixel* on in App embeds, and Save. It captures the views, clicks and CTR that power ranking; the *events received* line confirms it's live. **Step 3** then turns on top-sellers tracking with one click.

<Callout type="note" title="A consent banner changes what arrives">
The pixel honours your store's customer-privacy settings. Until a shopper grants **analytics** consent, QSortby records only that a visit happened — no views, clicks or cart events — so those shoppers contribute nothing to ranking and see the same order as everyone else. If *events received* looks low for your traffic, check what share of visitors accepts analytics before assuming the pixel is broken.
</Callout>

## 5. Move between steps — or skip for now

![Move between steps — or skip for now](/assets/setup-wizard/getting-started-setup-step-05.png)

The **Checklist** on the right lists all five steps and marks each as you finish it — click any row to jump straight to it. The two optional steps show as done from the outset, so read the checklist as "what's still blocking me" rather than a to-do list. Not ready to finish everything? **Skip for now** exits the wizard; you can always resume later from the Dashboard.
