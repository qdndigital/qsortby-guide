Most of this guide describes what a feature *does*. This page is the other half: the numbers the app **enforces**, so that when something says *Over plan limit* you can see why without opening a support ticket.

Every figure here is the app's own default for that plan. Prices live on the [pricing page](https://qsortby.com/pricing) and in the Shopify App Store listing — deliberately not here, so there is only ever one place to change them.

## The three that surprise people

**The ranking-window floor on Free.** Free pins the shortest window to **7 days**. It is a floor, not a fixed setting — a Free store can rank on the last 30 days or the last year, it just cannot pick "last 24 hours". The reason is statistical rather than commercial: at a small store's order volume a one-day window is mostly noise, and a collection that reshuffles daily on two orders looks broken. Every paid plan removes the floor, down to one hour.

**Goes-with rules are free to write, paid to publish.** Every plan can write Goes-with rules and apply them to a sorted collection's order. Writing the pairs onto your products as metafields — so your theme or another app can read them — starts on Starter. See [Publish to a product metafield](/docs/goes-with/publish-to-metafields).

**A/B testing is on Free with no cap, and on Starter it is one at a time.** That is not a typo: Free carries the uncapped experiments it has always had, Starter adds personalization and more collections but runs one experiment at a time, and Pro lifts the cap again. If you are on Starter and the **New experiment** button is disabled, a test is already running — finish or stop it first.

## What happens when you hit a cap

Nothing is deleted, and nothing is hidden. The pattern is the same everywhere in the app:

- **Collections** beyond the cap stay in your list and stop updating; the order already on your storefront stays. Pause or remove one and the next resumes on its normal schedule.
- **Upsell offers** beyond the cap stay configured and stop rendering, marked **Over plan limit**. Where a type is capped by count, the ones highest in your priority order are the ones that keep serving — so the order you set decides what survives a downgrade. See [Upsell offers](/docs/upsell/upsell-overview).
- **Display rules** already attached to an offer keep being evaluated. You just cannot create a new one or attach one that is not already attached; QSortby would rather leave a gate in place than quietly widen who sees an offer.
- **Tabs** for a tier you are not on — Intent in Results, AI connections in Settings — stay where they are and show what the tier would add rather than disappearing.
- **One exception:** the related-product metafields are deleted from your products when the plan no longer includes them, so your theme never renders a list that has stopped updating. See [Storefront data](/docs/settings/storefront-data).

Starting a paid plan restores everything exactly as it was.

## Before you pick a tier

Every new install runs on the **Growth** feature set for its first 10 days, so you can measure the surfaces rather than read about them. [Your first 10 days](/docs/getting-started/reverse-trial) explains what changes on day 11 — and suggests deciding from your own [Impact numbers](/docs/results/impact) around day seven, which beats choosing from any feature table, this one included.
