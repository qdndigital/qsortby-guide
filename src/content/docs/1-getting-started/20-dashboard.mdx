The Dashboard is your home base in QSortby. It shows real-time performance for the products you track, how far along your setup is, and which personalize rules are currently live — so you always know what's working at a glance.

## 1. Open the Dashboard

![Open the Dashboard](/assets/dashboard/getting-started-dashboard-step-01.png)

**Dashboard** is the first item in the left menu. It opens automatically after you install QSortby, and you can return to it any time.

## 2. Read your key metrics

![Read your key metrics](/assets/dashboard/getting-started-dashboard-step-02.png)

The four cards at the top are your live pulse: **Products tracked** (with how many are in stock), **Units sold** over the last 24 hours, **Products with sales**, and **Last inventory sync**. Only the sales figures carry that 24-hour window — the catalogue counts and the sync time come from your Shopify sync, not from storefront events.

## 3. Finish setup & see live rules

![Finish setup & see live rules](/assets/dashboard/getting-started-dashboard-step-03.png)

**Getting started** tracks five milestones with a progress bar — industry chosen, store data connected, event tracking on, first sort rule live, plan picked. It's a slightly different list from the [setup wizard](/docs/getting-started/setup-wizard)'s, because it checks the *outcome* rather than the step. Click **Continue setup** in the card, or **Resume setup** in the banner above it, to go back to the wizard. On the right, **Active rules** lists your personalize logics with a **Published** or **Draft** badge; click one to edit it.
