qSortBy automatically reorders your Shopify collections by what's actually selling — best sellers, CTR, revenue or trending — and keeps them fresh as new orders come in. This quick start gives you a tour of the app, then walks you through creating and sorting your first collection.

## 1. Get your bearings

![Get your bearings](/assets/quickstart/qsortby-quickstart-step-01.png)

The **Dashboard** is your home base. The tiles up top track how qSortBy is performing — collections under management, average CTR, auto-sorts run, and revenue influenced. Use the left navigation to move between **Collections**, **Analytics**, **Insights**, and **Settings**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
These are mocked preview numbers — your store's real metrics appear once the app is installed.
</Callout>

## 2. Browse your collections

![Browse your collections](/assets/quickstart/qsortby-quickstart-step-02.png)

Open **Collections** to see everything qSortBy manages. Each row shows its live **Sort Rule**, schedule, and 30-day CTR at a glance. To make a new one, select **Create collection** in the top-right.

## 3. Set up the collection

![Set up the collection](/assets/quickstart/qsortby-quickstart-step-03.png)

Give the collection a name, then choose how products join it — **manually**, where you pick each product, or **automatically** by conditions like tag, type or price. qSortBy creates a real Shopify collection in your store.

## 4. Choose how it sorts

![Choose how it sorts](/assets/quickstart/qsortby-quickstart-step-04.png)

Now build the sort rule: **(1)** pick a signal under *Sort products by* — **Best sellers** ranks by units sold — **(2)** choose the **sales window** it measures, and **(3)** turn on **Update automatically** to re-sort on a schedule. The **In plain words** panel restates your choices in a sentence. When you're happy, select **Create collection**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
Set updates to "As sales come in" for live re-ranking, or Daily/Hourly for a steadier cadence.
</Callout>

## 5. See it re-rank before going live

![See it re-rank before going live](/assets/quickstart/qsortby-quickstart-step-05.png)

On any collection, the **Preview & test** tab does a dry run — it shows your **current** order beside the **proposed** order, with ▲/▼ badges for how far each product moves. Nothing publishes to your storefront until you apply the sort, so you can check the result risk-free.

That's the full loop: tour the dashboard, create a collection, build a sort rule, and preview the re-rank. Once you apply the sort, qSortBy keeps the collection ordered automatically as new sales come in.
