Most of this guide describes what a feature *does*. This page is the other half: the numbers the app **enforces**, so that when something says *Over plan limit* you can see why without opening a support ticket.

Every figure here is the app's own default for that plan. Prices live on the [pricing page](https://qsortby.com/pricing) and in the Shopify App Store listing — deliberately not here, so there is only ever one place to change them.

## The three that surprise people

**The ranking-window floor on Free.** Free pins the shortest window to **7 days**. It is a floor, not a fixed setting — a Free store can rank on the last 30 days or the last year, it just cannot pick "last 24 hours". The reason is statistical rather than commercial: at Free's order volume a one-day window is mostly noise, and a collection that reshuffles daily on two orders looks broken. Every paid tier removes the floor, down to a **1-hour** window. See [Window and schedule](/docs/collections/window-and-schedule).

**Orders per month is a nudge, not a wall.** Cross 200 on Free (or 2,000 on Starter) and ranking keeps running exactly as before. You will see an upgrade prompt in the dashboard; nothing on your storefront changes and no collection stops syncing. QSortby does not break a live storefront to sell a plan.

**A/B testing is on every plan, with no cap on how many run at once.** Including Free. If you have read otherwise anywhere, that source is out of date — experiments carry no per-plan limit in the app.

<Callout type="note" title="These are defaults, not the last word">
Plan tiers set the baseline. QSortby can grant or withhold an individual capability for one store on top of whatever its plan says, so if a limit behaves differently from this table for your shop, that override is the likeliest reason — ask and we'll tell you what's set.
</Callout>

## What happens when you hit a cap

Nothing is deleted, and nothing is hidden. The pattern is the same everywhere in the app:

- **Collections** beyond the cap stay in your list and stop syncing. Delete or pause one and the next resumes on its normal schedule.
- **Upsell widgets** beyond the cap stay configured and stop rendering, marked **Over plan limit**. Where a type is capped by count, the ones highest in your priority order are the ones that keep serving — so the order you set decides what survives a downgrade. See [Upsell offers](/docs/upsell/upsell-overview).
- **Display rules** already attached to an offer keep being evaluated. You just cannot create a new one or attach one that is not already attached; QSortby would rather leave a gate in place than quietly widen who sees an offer.
- **Pages** for a tier you are not on — Analytics, Insights — stay in the menu and show what the tier would add rather than disappearing.

Re-subscribing restores everything exactly as it was.

## Before you pick a tier

Every new install runs on the **Growth** feature set for its first 10 days, so you can measure the surfaces rather than read about them. [Your first 10 days](/docs/getting-started/reverse-trial) explains what changes on day 11 — and suggests deciding from your own [attribution numbers](/docs/analytics/experiments-attribution) around day seven, which beats choosing from any feature table, this one included.
