Installation is handled entirely through your store's admin — no code, no theme edits.

## Steps

1. Open the app listing and click **Add to store**.
2. Review the requested permissions and approve them.
3. You'll land back in your admin on the app's **Setup** screen.
4. Pick the collections you want the app to manage, then click **Enable**.

<Callout type="note">
The app only reads and reorders the collections you enable. Everything else is left exactly as it is.
</Callout>

## Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | Any paid store plan |
| Permissions | Read products, manage collections |
| Setup time | ~2 minutes |

## Verify it's working

Open one of the collections you enabled on your live storefront. Within a few minutes you should see the order update. If it doesn't, see the [FAQ](/docs/help/faq#its-not-updating).

<Callout type="warning" title="Give it a moment">
The first sort can take a few minutes while the app reads your recent sales history. After that, updates are near real-time.
</Callout>
