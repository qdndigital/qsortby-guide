Welcome 👋 This guide walks you through installing, configuring and getting the most out of the app. Every page is written in Markdown — so it stays easy to update and easy to read.

## Who this is for

Store owners and teams who want their catalog working harder, without touching theme code. No technical background needed.

<Callout type="tip" title="New here?">
Start with **[Install the app](/docs/getting-started/install)** — it takes about two minutes. Then read **[How it works](/docs/using-the-app/how-it-works)** to understand what happens behind the scenes.
</Callout>

## How the guide is organized

- **Getting started** — install and first-run setup.
- **Using the app** — day-to-day features and how they behave.
- **Help** — answers to common questions.

Use the sidebar on the left to jump around, and the outline on the right to move within a page.
