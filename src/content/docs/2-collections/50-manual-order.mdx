Sometimes you want the final say on order — a hero product up top, a seasonal push, a merchandising call an algorithm can't make. The Manual order tab lets you arrange products by hand while still showing the metrics behind each one.

## 1. Open the Manual order tab

![Open the Manual order tab](/assets/manual-order/qsortby-manual-order-step-01.png)

Left menu → **Collections** → open a collection → the **Manual order** tab. It lists every product in the collection as a table, in the order shoppers currently see.

## 2. Arrange products by hand

![Arrange products by hand](/assets/manual-order/qsortby-manual-order-step-02.png)

Reorder the rows to set the exact sequence you want. This is where you override the algorithm — pin a launch product to the top, or group a promotion together, regardless of what the sales signals suggest.

## 3. Let the metrics guide you

![Let the metrics guide you](/assets/manual-order/qsortby-manual-order-step-03.png)

Each row shows per-product metrics — sales, CTR, and inventory — right beside the product. Use them as a guide while you curate, so your hand-set order still respects what's actually performing.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
Switch the sort rule to Manual pinning if you want this hand-set order to stick instead of being re-sorted automatically.
</Callout>

Manual order gives you full control: arrange products exactly as you want them, with per-product metrics on hand to keep your curation grounded in real data.
