A collection in QSortby is a Shopify collection that sorts itself by a rule you choose — best sellers, trending, top rated, and more. This guide creates one on the **Create collection** page. The app recomputes the order every 5 minutes so it always reflects live behaviour.

## 1. Open Collections and start a new one

![Open Collections and start a new one](/assets/create-collection/collections-create-step-01.png)

Open **Collections** from the left menu, then click **Create collection** (top right). It opens the setup page. The tiles above the list count your collections by state — **Live**, **Needs setup**, **Paused**.

## 2. Details — name it and pick a ranking rule

![Details — name it and pick a ranking rule](/assets/create-collection/collections-create-step-02.png)

Under **Details**, give the collection a shopper-facing **Collection title** (e.g. “Best sellers — last 7 days”). Then choose a **Ranking criteria** — the rule that decides which products go in and how they're ordered (Best selling, Trending, Top rated, and more). Set the **Mode / Unit / Value** window it measures over. Each rule is explained in the *How QSortby ranks* guides.

## 3. Products — how they get in

![Products — how they get in](/assets/create-collection/collections-create-step-03.png)

Under **Products**, pick **Algorithmic** (the app picks products by your criteria every 5 minutes) or **Manual** (you add products by hand; the app only reorders them). **Max products** caps how many the app promotes (1–250; ignored in Manual mode). An optional **Category filter** limits ranking to one category.

## 4. Create it

![Create it](/assets/create-collection/collections-create-step-04.png)

Click **Create collection**. QSortby makes the Shopify collection and starts keeping it in sync — recomputing and resyncing every 5 minutes. It appears in your list as **Needs setup** until the first sync finishes, then flips to **Live**.
