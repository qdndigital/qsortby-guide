Creating a collection in qSortBy takes three decisions: what to call it, how products join it, and how it sorts. This guide walks through each one so your new collection is live and re-ranking from day one.

## 1. Name the collection

![Name the collection](/assets/create-collection/qsortby-collections-create-step-01.png)

Open **Collections** from the left menu, then select **Create collection**. Give the collection a clear, shopper-facing name in the top field — this is the title customers see on your storefront. qSortBy creates a real Shopify collection in your store under this name.

## 2. Choose how products are added

![Choose how products are added](/assets/create-collection/qsortby-collections-create-step-02.png)

Under **How are products added?**, pick the membership type. **Manual** lets you hand-pick each product. **Automatic** builds the collection from conditions like tag, product type, or price, so new matching products join on their own. You can always switch later.

## 3. Pick the sort signal

![Pick the sort signal](/assets/create-collection/qsortby-collections-create-step-03.png)

Under **Sort products by**, choose the signal qSortBy uses to rank the collection. **Best sellers** orders by units sold; other options rank by CTR, revenue, how new a product is, inventory, or trending momentum. This is the heart of the sort rule.

## 4. Confirm and create

![Confirm and create](/assets/create-collection/qsortby-collections-create-step-04.png)

The **In plain words** panel restates your choices in a single sentence so you can sanity-check the rule before saving. When it reads the way you want, select **Create collection** to save it and start sorting.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
You can revisit the sort rule any time from the collection's Setup tab.
</Callout>

That's a collection created: named, populated by manual or automatic membership, and wired to a sort signal. qSortBy now keeps it ordered for you.
