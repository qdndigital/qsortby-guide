A collection in QSortby is a Shopify collection that sorts itself by a rule you choose — best sellers, trending, top revenue, and more. This guide creates one on the **Create collection** page. By default the app recomputes the order every 5 minutes so it always reflects live behaviour; you can slow that down, or turn automatic updates off entirely, in the **Keep updated** section.

## 1. Open Collections and start a new one

![Open Collections and start a new one](/assets/create-collection/collections-create-step-01.png)

Open **Collections** from the left menu, then click **Create collection** (top right). It opens the setup page. The tiles above the list count your collections in total and by state — **Live**, **Needs setup**, **Paused**.

## 2. Details — name it and pick a ranking rule

![Details — name it and pick a ranking rule](/assets/create-collection/collections-create-step-02.png)

Under **Details**, give the collection a shopper-facing **Collection title** (e.g. “Best sellers — last 7 days”). Then choose a **Ranking criteria** — the rule that decides which products go in and how they're ordered (Best selling, Top revenue, Trending, Newest arrivals, and more; **Top rated** is marked *coming soon* here and can only be set later from the collection's own page). Set the **Mode / Unit / Value** window it measures over — that picker is hidden for the rules that don't use a window, namely Most profitable, Customer favorites and Personalize. Each rule is explained in the *How QSortby ranks* guides.

## 3. Products — how they get in

![Products — how they get in](/assets/create-collection/collections-create-step-03.png)

Under **Products**, pick **Algorithmic** (the app picks products by your criteria on every sync) or **Manual** (you add products by hand; the app only reorders them). Only products that are **Active** and **published to the Online Store** are eligible — drafts and archived products are never promoted, while sold-out products stay eligible and sink to the bottom. **Max products** caps how many the app promotes (1–250; ignored in Manual mode). An optional **Category filter** limits ranking to one category, matched on the product's category metafield — **Best selling** is the exception, matching on the older tag-based category field and case-sensitively, so a Best-selling collection with a category filter can come back short or empty where other rules find plenty.

## 4. Keep updated, then create it

![Keep updated, then create it](/assets/create-collection/collections-create-step-04.png)

Under **Keep updated**, leave **Update automatically** on and set **How often** (every 5, 15 or 30 minutes, hourly, every 6 hours, or a custom 5–1440 minutes); **Push to Shopify automatically** ships each new order straight to your store, or stages it for you to review. Then click **Create collection**. QSortby makes the Shopify collection and starts keeping it in sync on that schedule. It appears in your list as **Live** — or as **Paused** if you turned automatic updates off.
