## Does it change my theme code?

No. Everything happens through the store's collection API. Your theme is never touched, so there's nothing to undo if you uninstall.

## How fast do changes appear?

The first sort can take a few minutes; after that, re-ranking is near real-time as orders come in.

## It's not updating

A few things to check:

1. The collection is **enabled** in the app's Setup screen.
2. The collection has enough recent sales for a signal — brand-new collections need a little traffic first.
3. You're looking at the live storefront, not a cached preview.

<Callout type="danger" title="Still stuck?">
Email **hi@qdn.vn** with your store URL and the collection name and we'll investigate.
</Callout>

## Can I keep a product at the top?

Yes — **pin** it. Pinned products stay first regardless of their score. See [How it works](/docs/using-the-app/how-it-works#pinning-and-excluding).

## What happens if I uninstall?

The app stops reordering immediately and leaves your collections in their current order. No cleanup needed.
